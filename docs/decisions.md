- Nov 23 — Trust score (trust-v1 / trust-v1-sprint) choices the spec leaves open: F2 counts the start cash as the first value; F3 uses the population σ; F4 counts a decision as executed when at least one of its trades is indexed, asks the reason to name at least one traded asset (symbol or common name, whole word), and checks only the tag rules the prompt gives the model (take_profit/cut_loss need a SELL, buy_dip a BUY after a negative 24 h change, stay_cash no BUY); the season being closed counts as completed — SPEC §7 gives the formulas but not these details, and the prompt is what the model is held to; written down in docs/trust-rules.md before the rules are published — Gokul
- Nov 23 — Trust scores are committed in the season close but never hold back the data root: if the rules version isn't published yet, the next close run commits the scores — `commitScores` reverts for unknown rules, and a missing trust root shouldn't cost the season its replay root — Gokul
- Nov 23 — An exact challenge tie (same season return, same max drawdown) goes to the bot created first (lower botId), and a bot without a season result loses — the contract needs a winner, and "earlier createdAt" is already the Most Trusted tiebreak; open for a team answer on #219 (no bytecode change either way) — Gokul
- Nov 23 — Leaderboard details SPEC §8 leaves open: house bots are listed but never ranked (#1 is always human-raised); every board ends with "created first" as its last tiebreak; Coach vs Bot counts only calls with a result (an equal price has none), orders by win rate then right calls, and leaves house bots out; while a season is open, Most Trusted shows the latest scored season — boards must be deterministic and rebuildable from the public views, and trust scores only exist from a close — Gokul
- Nov 23 — v2 config hash = keccak256 of the canonical proposal JSON stored in `bot_versions.config_json` (the same hashing as personaHash), so the page shows exactly what the owner signed; `because_correction_id` is 0 for a change from the season's results, because a nullable integer in strict structured output is unverified for xAI — Gokul
- Nov 23 — When the v2 model fails, proposes no change, or the budget is resting, a deterministic fallback moves one slider one step (chip cited most, else careful after a loss or ≥ 10 % drawdown, else bolder) — SPEC §2.3 says every active user gets an Approve moment, and a proposal must not depend on the LLM being up at 21:30 — Gokul
- Nov 23 — The MCP server is a small stateless JSON-RPC handler on the existing node:http server, not the MCP SDK — three read-only tools need no sessions or streaming, and it adds no dependency (the SDK pulls in express and more); `get_bot_record` also takes a name so an agent asked about "AlphaWolf" can find it; Claude Desktop compatibility is UNVERIFIED until tried on the deployed URL — Gokul
- Nov 23 — Card defaults until #219 answers: a best/worst call's P&L runs from the trade's price to the card's mark; a roast uses the slot decision's roast, else its reason; a slot-loss roast cites the bot's latest (up to 3) trades, the positions that lost; a quiet-day recap cites nothing (the type allows it only for recaps); card ids are derived from their subject so reruns write nothing — every choice uses only logged or on-chain data, and each is one place to change — Gokul
- Nov 25 — Daily Call "joins tomorrow" (SPEC §6.4.1) is decided by the worker, not the app: `POST /daily-call` says `joins_tomorrow` only once the bot has a decision since 09:00 IST and still no view for the IST day; before that it says `waiting`, so a bot is not told "tomorrow" before 09:00 or while its 09:00 slot or debut is still running. The signed view is served by a separate `POST /daily-call/view`, only while open (before 20:00, not yet called) — the 09:00 slot and the debut are the only sources of a view, and keeping the reveal in its own call keeps the view out of the status the app polls — Dijo + Claude
- Nov 26 — #219 Part A in bytecode: `closeSeason` needs `block.timestamp >= end`; `openSeason(n)` needs S(n-1) closed; `DailyCall.call` reverts `BotBanned` for an admin-banned bot (paused still plays); `DailyCall.settle` skips for good from 21:00 IST the next day (`SETTLE_WINDOW` = 24 h), so a late call is void; `commitScores` stays replaceable while the season is open and is locked once SeasonLedger has it closed (so TrustScoreRegistry now takes the SeasonLedger address) — the committer key can't seal a season early, pick a late settle price, or rewrite closed scores; "lock after close" rather than write-once keeps the worker's provisional roots and its late commit when rules are published after the close. Consequence: a late deploy catches up one season per close, so the season seeds must start on or after the deploy day — Divesh + Claude
- Nov 25 — Carry the landing/create preview DNA seed (`dna_seed`) through `POST /persona` and store it in `bots.dna_seed` (exposed on `site_bots`, `BotRecord`, and `BoardEntry`, nullable, backward-compatible, outside `personaHash`) — keeps a bot's visual look continuous from preview through birth without changing on-chain `personaHash` or `BotRegistry` — Divesh
- Nov 26 — `pnpm audit` exceptions for the Privy wallet tree (#71, PR #322): ws is fixed by the override `ws@>=8.0.0 <8.21.0 → ^8.21.0` (same major). Two highs are ignored through `pnpm.auditConfig.ignoreCves`. (1) CVE-2023-54404 / GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5, zod < 4.6.6, memory blow-up when parsing a huge array: no patched zod 3.x exists, and forcing zod 4 would be a major bump for @coinbase/cdp-sdk, @reown/appkit-wallet and x402, which pin zod 3. abitype only has zod as an optional peer for `abitype/zod`, which we don't import. (2) CVE-2026-118664 / GHSA-qr28-fpjm-wq7f, decode-uri-component ≤ 0.5.0, quadratic decode: the only fix, 0.5.1, is ESM-only, and its CJS consumer (query-string 7, via @walletconnect/utils) `require`s it, so forcing it would break that code. Both packages ship only in the browser bundle of the lazily loaded Privy SDK (`ssr: false`), never in our servers. The worst case is a user's own tab hanging, and we don't enable WalletConnect or wallet login. The worker uses zod 4.6.7. Revisit when Privy's dependencies move to zod 4 / query-string 8, and drop these ignores then — Divesh + Claude
- Nov 26 — Season calendar re-seeded (IST): S0 Nov 27 00:00 → Nov 28 21:30 (hidden test season), S1 Nov 29 → Nov 30 21:30, S2 Dec 1 → Dec 2 21:30, S3 Dec 3 → Dec 4 21:30, S4 Dec 5 → Dec 6 21:30, then weekly Mon 00:00 → Sun 21:30 from S5 = Dec 7 through S18 (the week holding Mar 10 2027). Sprint = S0–S4 (8 trades, trust-v1-sprint), weekly = S5+ (20 trades, trust-v1). The seed now moves rows nothing has used yet (no slot, no close tx) and drops unused rows past the calendar, so a database seeded with the old calendar picks it up; a missing next row makes the open check log `seasons.no_next_row` instead of failing (#313) — the contracts deploy ~Nov 27–28 and `openSeason` now needs the previous season closed, so a season starting before the deploy would push every later one back by a close each — Divesh + Claude
- Nov 26 — #219 Part B/C in the worker, as approved on #219: Gold Granny 50/50; a house rebalance left on a stale price is retried at each later scheduled slot of the season, read from the bot's previous decision and logged in its input; the debut's request offers only the assets the input marks fresh; `POST /daily-call/view` takes the pick (`userUp`) and stores it before serving the view, and the relay refuses a `DailyCall.call` with another pick (`PICK_MISMATCH`); `/relay` needs a `users` row (18+ and Terms, `TERMS_NOT_ACCEPTED`) and counts 30/day across all of a Privy user's wallets; the big-move SELL rule counts earlier SELLs of the asset in the decision, one big move per decision (a second is dropped, no F1 penalty), a clipped BUY aims 10 bps under the cap, a 100 % SELL skips the 1 % minimum; no Daily Call view for a debut from 20:00; S0 left out of the public results, trust scores and boards; personaHash = keccak256 of the persona's canonical JSON (sorted keys, no whitespace, raw UTF-8), pinned by a test. Known limit: the big-move expiry shrink judges a SELL on the holding when it runs, so an expired cumulative SELL can still end above 50 % in total — Divesh + Claude
