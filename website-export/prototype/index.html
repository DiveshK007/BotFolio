      What the oracle can and can't promise

      All five feeds are updated by one QIE-operated wallet. So our wording is exact: trades are settled against QIE's official mainnet oracle. We don't call that anything grander.

      Feeds sometimes go quiet. On Nov 19 we measured crypto gaps of up to 144 minutes and gold gaps of over 6 hours. When a price is too old, the bot holds that asset and says so ("Gold market quiet — holding"). It never trades on a stale price.

      Holding — price too old

      Under 60 min: muted. 60 min or more: amber. Past the limit (30 min crypto, 6 h gold): grey, no trade. EXAMPLE values

      Activity: people first, robots second

      User-signed txs are counted by the signer's address. System txs (runner batches, settlements, season roots) are listed apart and never added to user numbers.

      Trust score: 0 to 1000, four public rules

      Rules version trust-v1, published on-chain. Each factor is worth 250. New bots are marked "new" until they have 8 trades and one finished season (20 trades from Season 6).

      FactorWhat it measuresFormula
      Follows its rulesProposed actions that pass the risk gate unclipped250 × (1 − clipped / proposed)
      Drawdown controlSmall peak-to-trough drops250 × max(0, 1 − maxDD / 0.30)
      ConsistencyCalm daily returns250 × max(0, 1 − σ_daily / 0.04)
      Explains its tradesReasons that name the asset and match the action250 × valid / executed

      Replay a season yourself

      Each season gets a replay file with every decision, the logged AI output, the risk-gate result, oracle round ids and tx hashes. Its Merkle root is committed on QIE at season close.

      The honest claim: AI output isn't guaranteed to repeat, so we log it. Given the logged output, everything after it is deterministic and recomputable.

      Play money. Not investment advice. No prizes.
      
    
      BotFolio is a game about raising and coaching AI traders. Here's what that does and doesn't mean, in plain words.
      
  
      
  
      
    
      Play money
      Every bot gets ₹1,00,000 of play money. It isn't real, can't be topped up, and can't be cashed out. There's no token, no NFT, no entry fee and nothing to buy.
      
    
      Not advice
      Bots are AI characters making pretend trades. Nothing they say is a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and we never link to places that sell real assets.
      
    
      No prizes
      Leaderboards and seasons are for bragging rights. A "Bot of the Week" shoutout is the whole trophy cabinet. No stakes on challenges or daily calls.
      
  
      
  
      
    
      What we store
      Your wallet address and your login id. That's it. No phone number or email, unless you choose email login.
      
      
      What "on-chain" means here
      Your bot's creation, its trades, your coaching and its versions are recorded on QIE mainnet so anyone can check them. Gas is paid by BotFolio, so you never need crypto to play.
      
    
      Questions
      
      
      Why does the ₹ number look exact?
      On-chain values are in play USDT. We show ₹ as 1,00,000 × current value ÷ starting value, so it's exact for your bot. Any USDT-to-₹ figure in a tooltip is approximate.
      
      
      Who sets the prices?
      QIE's official mainnet oracle. The contract reads it at the moment of each trade. We never pass a price in.
      
      
      Can I lose real money?
      No. There is no real money anywhere in BotFolio.
